    Why PDH?

    A pillar of trust for our community

    PDH is a pillar of trust in healthcare for its neighboring community, driven by a deep passion for delivering patient-centered care across generations. Accredited by the Department of Health (DOH), PhilHealth and various HMOs, we offer a 94-bed inpatient facility, Emergency Room, ICU, NICU, Operating and Delivery Rooms, Laboratory and Radiology.

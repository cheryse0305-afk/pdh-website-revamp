    Why PDH?

    PDH is a pillar of trust in healthcare for its neighboring community, driven by a deep passion for delivering patient-centered care across generations. With a comprehensive range of medical facilities—including a 94-bed inpatient facility, Emergency Room (ER), Intensive Care Unit (ICU), Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU), Operating Room (OR), Delivery Room (DR), Laboratory, and Radiology, PDH is also accredited by the Department of Health (DOH), PhilHealth, and various Health Maintenance Organizations (HMOs), ensuring accessible healthcare and full compliance with government-regulated standards.

    Multi Diagnostic Center

    Our Multi Diagnostic Center provides a wide array of diagnostic services, including cardiology, neurology, vascular procedures, and more. Equipped with diagnostic tools like ECG, Echocardiograms, and EEG, this center is dedicated to offering accurate assessments to guide effective treatment plans.

    View Diagnostic Services

    Hemodialysis Center

    PDH’s Hemodialysis Center provides essential renal care for patients with kidney-related conditions. Our center offers dialysis treatment to individuals with chronic kidney disease, ensuring they receive the necessary care to manage their health.

    Visit the Hemodialysis Center

    Eye Center

    Our Eye Center is equipped to handle a variety of eye-related health concerns, including common and specialized eye diseases, from modern cataract surgery and microsurgery to LASIK/laser screening.
